Most entries are short.

Of the 9,094 subway records with a positive delay in 2025, 5,582 lasted five minutes or less. Switch the measure to see what those entries contribute to the year’s 70,754 recorded minutes.

Only 212 entries lasted half an hour or more. Together, they accounted for about one in five recorded minutes.

Weather climbs the list.

Weather ranked 14th among 117 codes by number of records. By minutes it ranked eighth. Three of its longest entries came on the same Sunday. Set those three aside and weather drops to 23rd.

Add the entries back in the order they were logged. The other 116 codes keep their full-year totals.

February 16 accounts for 2,075 weather minutes. These three entries alone account for 1,964, or 71.5% of the whole year’s weather total.

Back on the platform

The Eglinton entry tells us where and when the TTC logged a problem. Several entries can cover the same stretch of time. To estimate one rider’s wait, we would need to know when they reached the platform and when a train arrived.
